Chapter I • The Beginning

11th Standard Ki Woh Masoom Shuruat

Jab dosti aur pyaar ke darmiyaan ki lakeer mitne lagi thi, aur tum meri aadat ban gayi thi.

Yaad hai woh 11th standard ke din? Jab zindagi kitni simple aur masoom hua karti thi. School ke corridors, lectures ke beech chori-chupe ek doosre ko dekhna, aur bina kisi matlab ke bas tumhara sath hona hi din ka sabse khoobsurat hissa ban jaana.

"Hum dono ko andaza bhi nahi tha ki woh choti-choti baatein, woh halki si muskaan, aage chal kar meri zindagi ka sabse bada maksad ban jaayegi."

Maine kabhi socha nahi tha ki 11th class mein shuru hui yeh dosti ek din mujhe mere jeene ki wajah de degi. Tumhare chehre ki woh hasi mere har bure din ko theek kar deti thi. Hum bade ho rahe the, sapne bun rahe the, aur anjaane mein ek doosre ki taqdeer ban rahe the.

Meri Pyaari Manisha,
With all my love & sincerity

Aksar jab humare beech ladai hoti hai ya main galti karta hoon, tum sochtee ho ki "isse koi fark nahi padta, isse pachtaava nahi hai." Par Manisha, sach yeh hai ki mujhe andar hi andar itna guilt hota hai jo main lafzon mein bayaan nahi kar paata.

"Main apne emotions express karne mein hamesha kamzor raha hoon. Jab main chup rehta hoon, iska matlab yeh nahi ki main guilty nahi hoon—uska matlab yeh hai ki main khud se sharminda hoon ki maine us ladki ko dukh diya jise main sabse zyada chahta hoon."

11th standard ke us masoom waqt se lekar aaj tak, humne kitna lamba safar tay kiya hai. Long distance ne humare sabr ka imtehaan liya. Maine kayi baar bina soche bol diya, kayi baar tumhari baat ko samajhne mein der kar di, aur kayi galtiyan ki jinhe main waqt peeche karke theek karna chahta hoon.

Main aaj dil ki gehraiyon se tumse dil se maafi maangta hoon (I am truly, deeply sorry). Tum meri aane wali patni ho, meri zindagi ki sabse khoobsurat hakeeqat ho. Tumhara samman, tumhari khushi, aur tumhare chehre ki muskaan mere liye har cheez se badhkar hai.

Main vaada karta hoon ki main ek behtar insaan, ek behtar sathi, aur tumhara sabse sachha jeevansathi banunga. Mujhe maafi dekar ek baar phir mera haath thaam lo.

Tumhara Rahul
Your Upcoming Husband & Forever Believer
